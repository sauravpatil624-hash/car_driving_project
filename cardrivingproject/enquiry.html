Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries

What documents do I need?

You'll need a valid Aadhar Card, age proof, and two passport-size photographs. For those under 18, parent consent is required.

What is the minimum age?

The minimum age for car driving training is 18 years. You must be at least 18 at the time of enrollment.

Do you help with license?

Yes! Our Premium package includes full assistance with the driving license application process at the RTO.

Can I choose my timings?

Yes, Standard and Premium packages offer flexible timing options. You can choose morning, afternoon, or evening slots.
